A fire-themed content mod.

Foolish Flame introduces a 'heat' mechanic, and content centered around it.

Heat grants a passive damage boost and is also used as a currency of sorts for many of the mod's spells.
The more heat you have, the higher the damage boost & the more powerful your spells become!

Heat is acquired from many sources (including by holding kiuaskivi), but mainly from the mod's spells and by being around enemies afflicted with Magic Fire.

Magic Fire is a new status effect that is inflicted by many of the mod's spells and has 10 temperature levels.
The higher the temperature, the more heat is gained & the more powerful some of the mod's spells are against that enemy.

How much heat you have is shown by a display that appears above the player.
The heat gauge can take multiple appearances, which can be cycled using the [b][[/b] and [b]][/b] keys in game.
